In Loving Memory of our BHS '82 Classmates

As we celebrate the memories of our school days at Balboa High School in the Canal Zone, Panama, and the friendships that have lasted through the years, we also take a moment to remember those classmates who are no longer with us.

Although they are no longer here they will always remain a part of our Balboa High School Class of 1982 and a part of our story. We remember their smiles, their laughter, their personalities, and the special moments we shared together at school, the causeway, the Yacht Club, the beach, etc. Each of them touched our lives in their own unique way, and their absence is deeply felt.

Time may have passed, but memories have a way of keeping people close. May we honor and remember our classmates with love and gratitude. They may no longer walk beside us, but they will always have a place in our hearts and in the memories we carry with us.
